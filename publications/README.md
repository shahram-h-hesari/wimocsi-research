# WiMoCSI Research Publications

WiMoCSI research investigates physics-based Wi-Fi sensing, human motion detection, walking-speed estimation, and measurement reliability.

This directory will provide verified publication records and links to scientific materials approved for public distribution.

## Research Topics

- Physics-based Wi-Fi channel modeling
- Human motion detection
- Walking-speed estimation using commodity Wi-Fi CSI
- Wireless sensing observability and recoverability
- Measurement uncertainty and acquisition robustness
- Evidence-aware wireless sensing

## Publication Records

Verified conference and journal publication records will be added following author approval and bibliographic verification.

Publication entries will identify the paper title, authors, venue, year, publication status, and available DOI or official publication link.
